Workplace Safety & MultiplyOS Standard Operating Procedures MultiplyOS Aligned
1. Required PPE Compliance

- ANSI Z87.1 safety glasses required in shop and field.
- Kevlar sleeves and cut-resistant gloves for raw glass.
- Closed-toe safety footwear required on all routes.

2. Fleet & Mobile Rig SOP

- Complete 35-point Weekly Vehicle Inspection prior to route.
- Verify glass racks, tie-downs, and vacuum cups daily.
- Flagged vehicle defects automatically alert Operations.

3. MultiplyOS Handbook Sign-Off

- Clock-in/out requires high-precision street geotagging.
- Full-Time (>35h) and Part-Time (>25h) require supervisor approval prior to exceeding cap.
- Log all completed WOs daily for scoreboard accuracy.
