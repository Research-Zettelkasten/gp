Disconnect QuickBooks Online

Secure account-management instructions · GLASSPRO Accounting Bridge

Only an authorized GLASSPRO administrator can disconnect the company's QuickBooks integration from within the portal. This public information page does not itself revoke access or change QuickBooks data.

Disconnect through GLASSPRO

  1. Sign in to GLASSPRO using your administrator account.
  2. Open Administration → Management → MultiplyOS Hub.
  3. Find QuickBooks Online · Secure Account Connection.
  4. Choose Disconnect from QuickBooks and confirm. The server asks Intuit to revoke authorization before removing the stored connection.
  5. Return to the QBO connection status panel to confirm that it reads Not connected.
Open Management / QBO settings

If you cannot access GLASSPRO

A QuickBooks company administrator can also manage authorized applications from within QuickBooks Online using its app management tools. If you revoke GLASSPRO's access from QuickBooks, ask the GLASSPRO administrator to check that the old connection is no longer treated as active. Do not share Intuit account passwords or API secrets with anyone for this process.

What happens to existing information?

Disconnecting stops future access to the connected QuickBooks company. It does not delete invoices, payments, or customer information in QuickBooks itself, and it does not automatically erase historical QBO snapshots, collection notes, or reconciliation reports already stored in GLASSPRO. Contact your administrator if historical GLASSPRO records require review or lawful deletion.
