GLASSPRO by American Glass ("GLASSPRO", "we", "our") operates an internal software portal for authorized employees and administrators. This policy explains information handled through the portal, including its optional QuickBooks Online ("QBO") integration. Operating legal entity: CONFIRM_LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and personnel records: employee names, email addresses, login identifiers, roles and permissions, time punches, lunch start/end times, timecard reviews, and work or activity logs.
  • Operational data: quotes, work orders, vehicle inspections, relevant geotags collected by enabled features, customer follow-up and collection notes, and audit history.
  • Accounting data: customer names and contact details, invoice identifiers and balances, payment and credit records, and reconciliation status. These may arrive through authorized QBO API access or Mainstreet/QuickBooks CSV imports.
  • Technical information: limited session, access-control, and error information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the portal.

2. Why we use information

We use these records to operate the business, manage employee work and attendance, coordinate customer service and collections, investigate differences between accounting systems, administer access, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations. Collection notes are intended for authorized operational use, not public publication.

3. QuickBooks Online access

When an authorized administrator connects QBO, the GLASSPRO integration requests Intuit's com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting OAuth permission. The current integration uses API calls to retrieve customer, invoice, payment, and credit-memo information and store reconciliation snapshots in GLASSPRO's protected database. The current implementation does not intentionally create or edit QuickBooks transactions. The OAuth scope itself is broader than read-only access; this statement describes the app's implemented behavior, not an Intuit-enforced read-only permission.

Connection credentials are processed on the Supabase server-side integration rather than supplied to employees in browser JavaScript. Only authorized staff should be able to see business records according to their assigned portal permissions.

4. Providers and disclosures

We use service providers to operate this software, including Netlify for public website delivery, Supabase for authentication, database storage and server-side functions, and Intuit for authorized QuickBooks access. Information may be accessible to authorized administrators, employees assigned to relevant work, and service providers where necessary for the stated purposes. We do not use this portal to publish customer or employee business records publicly.

5. Retention and accounting snapshots

Imported accounting snapshots, collection histories, and audit records may remain in GLASSPRO after a QuickBooks account is disconnected. Disconnecting stops future QBO access but does not automatically remove historical imported records needed for accounting review, business operations, or lawful recordkeeping. An authorized administrator must separately evaluate and perform any appropriate deletion or export. Actual retention periods and deletion practices should be documented by the business before this policy is finalized.

6. Security

GLASSPRO uses signed-in accounts and database access policies to restrict portal information to authorized personnel. The QBO connector uses server-side functions for OAuth and syncing. No system is entirely risk-free; suspected unauthorized access should be reported promptly to the designated business contact. The operator must verify its production token encryption, access reviews, backup practices, and incident response procedures before claiming that they meet Intuit's requirements.

7. Access, correction, and questions

For questions about records, correction requests, or an appropriate retention/deletion review, contact the GLASSPRO administrator. Where applicable law gives individuals rights regarding their personal information, requests will be evaluated under that law and legitimate business recordkeeping needs.
