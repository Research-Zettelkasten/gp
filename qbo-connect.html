Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks Online

This page is a starting point for authorized GLASSPRO administrators. It does not ask for your Intuit password or receive authorization tokens.

  1. Sign in to GLASSPRO with an active administrator account.
  2. Open Administration → Management → MultiplyOS Hub.
  3. In the QuickBooks Online connection panel, select Connect / Reconnect QuickBooks.
  4. At the official Intuit authorization screen, choose the correct QuickBooks company and approve the requested access.
  5. Return to GLASSPRO and use Sync QBO Now to retrieve the first read-only accounting snapshot.
