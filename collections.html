How payment verification works

Reports are dated snapshots. A missing invoice in a complete QBO Collections Report enters the verification queue. Upload QBO Invoice List (all dates; paid and unpaid; Customer / Name, Num, Date, Amount, Open Balance, A/R Paid) to verify an invoice's remaining balance. A verified zero balance can be cleared with an audited note; it may reflect a payment, credit or write-off. Customer totals and unlinked payments never clear an invoice.
